# ClaimPilot voice-over script (~2:45)

Read each scene into its **own audio file**, named `scene1.m4a` … `scene8.m4a`.
Calm pace, short pause at each full stop. Don't worry about timing: the screen recording is cut to fit your audio.
A fluff? Just re-record that one scene.

---

**Scene 1 — The problem** (~15 s)
> Filing an expense claim takes fifteen to twenty-five minutes. You collect bills, type in the details, and guess what the policy allows. Then Finance sends it back for a missing GST bill or a duplicate. ClaimPilot does this work for you in about a minute.

**Scene 2 — Adding the bills** (~20 s)
> Here is a two-day client trip to Mumbai. Cab rides, meals, a hotel bill and its UPI payment, a handwritten fuel bill, an office supplies bill, and money sent to a colleague. I add all thirteen. The AI reads every bill and fills in the details by itself.

**Scene 3 — Clear reasons for every decision** (~30 s)
> For every value, I can see exactly where on the bill it was read from. The AI only reads the bills. Fixed company rules make every decision. The hotel's UPI payment is matched to its bill, so it is not counted twice. The office supplies bill is stopped because its GST number is not valid. Money sent to a person is not allowed. And when a meal is over the limit, "Why?" shows the exact line from the company policy.

**Scene 4 — Only the questions that matter** (~30 s)
> For thirteen bills, it asks only three questions. A date that can't be read, a cab bill that was uploaded twice, and a reason for the costly meal. If I just type "no", it is not accepted, because that explains nothing. A proper answer goes through. One last question about the fuel trip, and the claim is ready.

**Scene 5 — Submit, or ask for a review** (~20 s)
> I send it to Finance and get a reference number. If a bill was rejected and I think that is wrong, I can raise a ticket. A person in Finance will then review it, along with my note.

**Scene 6 — The Finance team's view** (~20 s)
> The Finance team gets a live dashboard. How many claims needed no questions at all, the cost per claim in rupees, the time saved, and the money saved by catching duplicates. All measured from every claim it processes.

**Scene 7 — We test it properly** (~25 s)
> We tested it on sixty-eight sample Indian bills: blurry, tilted, handwritten, and some uploaded twice. It got every detail, every duplicate and every policy check right. Every change is re-tested automatically. And the policy is written in plain English, then turned into rules, each linked to the exact policy line.

**Scene 8 — Close** (~10 s)
> It is not tied to any one AI company, and the online demo needs no paid keys. ClaimPilot: from a pile of bills to a claim that is ready to submit.

---

**Total:** about 390 words, about 2 min 45 s at a natural pace.
