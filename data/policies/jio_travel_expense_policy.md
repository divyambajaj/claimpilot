# Jio Platforms Limited — Travel and Expense Policy

Version 3.2, effective 1 April 2026. Owner: Finance Controllership.

## 1. Purpose and scope

### 1.1 Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses Jio Platforms Limited ("the Company") reimburses, how employees should claim them, and what evidence Finance needs to approve a claim quickly. The aim is simple: employees should never be out of pocket for legitimate business spending, and the Company should be able to show any auditor exactly why each rupee was paid.

### 1.2 Scope

The policy applies to all permanent employees, fixed-term employees and interns on the India payroll. Consultants and vendors are reimbursed under their contracts and are out of scope. Where a client contract sets stricter limits for a project, the client limits apply to expenses charged to that project.

## 2. General principles

### 2.1 Eligibility

An expense is reimbursable only if it was incurred wholly and necessarily for Company business, was paid by the employee personally, and has not been reimbursed by anyone else. Every expense needs a business purpose. An expense that does not fall into one of the categories in sections 4 to 6 must carry a short note explaining its business purpose. Employees should spend Company money as carefully as they would spend their own and choose the most economical option that still allows them to do their job well.

### 2.2 Approvals

Every claim is approved by the employee's line manager before it reaches Finance. Managers confirm the business purpose; Finance confirms compliance with this policy. A manager may not approve their own expenses, and claims above INR 50,000 in total also need approval from the department head.

### 2.3 Timelines

Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Late claims slow down month-end closing and make it harder to verify the expense, so a claim submitted after this window needs a short written justification. Expenses older than 90 days will not be reimbursed. Finance pays approved claims in the next weekly payment run, normally within seven working days of approval.

### 2.4 Currency

Claims are paid in Indian rupees. Expenses paid in a foreign currency are converted at the rate shown on the employee's card statement or, for cash, at the rate on the date of the expense published by the Company's bank. Conversion charges levied by the card issuer are reimbursable when supported by the statement.

## 3. Documentation

### 3.1 Documentation

Every expense must be supported by an original, legible bill or receipt showing the vendor name, the date, the amount and what was purchased. Photographs of bills are acceptable if all four corners and every printed figure are clearly visible. A payment confirmation on its own, such as a UPI success screen or a card slip, is not a bill. It proves that money was paid, not what was bought, so it must be accompanied by the bill or invoice for the same expense. Any single expense of INR 5,000 or more must be supported by a GST tax invoice bearing the vendor's GSTIN. Employees should ask the vendor for a tax invoice in the Company's name at the time of purchase, because it is rarely possible to obtain one later. Finance validates each GSTIN before payment.

### 3.2 Proof of payment

A bill shows what was bought; a payment confirmation shows that the employee paid for it. For digital payments above INR 2,000, attach the payment confirmation. A UPI success screen, card slip or bank statement extract is sufficient, provided it shows the amount, the date and the payee. Cash payments above INR 2,000 require a written justification, since they cannot be independently verified.

### 3.3 Duplicate and split claims

Each expense may be claimed only once. Submitting the same bill twice, or claiming both a bill and its payment confirmation as separate expenses, is treated as a duplicate claim. Splitting a single expense across several bills or several claims to stay under a limit is not permitted. The claims system checks every new bill against the employee's earlier claims, and suspected duplicates are held until the employee confirms or explains them.

## 4. Travel and meals

### 4.1 Local conveyance

Employees may use app-based cabs, auto-rickshaws, metro and local trains for business travel within a city. Local conveyance (cabs, auto-rickshaws and metro) is reimbursed up to INR 1,500 per day. Travel between home and the employee's regular office is a personal expense and is not reimbursable, except for late-night travel after 10 pm when the employee was required to stay back.

### 4.2 Meals

Meals with clients, partners or colleagues during business travel or approved working sessions are reimbursable. Meals are reimbursed up to INR 750 per person per meal. The bill or the claim must state how many people attended. Where a meal costs more than the limit, the Company reimburses up to the limit and the employee bears the difference unless the department head has approved an exception in advance, for example for a formal client dinner.

### 4.3 Intercity travel

Intercity travel should be booked through the Company travel desk wherever possible. Employees travel by economy class on domestic flights and by AC two-tier or AC chair car on trains. Tickets booked directly by an employee are reimbursed at the fare actually paid, provided the booking was made at least seven days before travel or the trip was urgent.

### 4.4 Fuel

Employees who use a personal vehicle for Company business may claim fuel. Fuel for personal vehicles used on company business is reimbursed up to INR 5,000 per claim. Every fuel claim must include the fuel station bill and a trip note stating the business purpose, the date and the places visited.

## 5. Lodging

### 5.1 Lodging

Employees travelling overnight on business may stay in a business hotel or a serviced apartment. Hotel stays are capped at INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 elsewhere. For this policy, metro cities are Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata and Pune. The cap covers the room tariff and taxes; breakfast included in the tariff is acceptable, but minibar, laundry and entertainment charges are personal.

### 5.2 Extended stays

For stays longer than seven nights in the same city, employees should ask the travel desk to arrange a serviced apartment at a negotiated rate. The nightly cap in section 5.1 continues to apply.

## 6. Other expenses

### 6.1 Office supplies

Stationery and small office supplies needed urgently may be bought locally and claimed. Office supplies bought on a weekend need a note from the manager explaining why they could not wait until a working day. Items that will be used for more than a year, such as electronics and furniture, must be bought through Procurement and are not reimbursable through expense claims.

### 6.2 Mobile and internet

Employees whose role requires them to be reachable outside office hours may claim their mobile or home internet bill. Mobile and internet bills are reimbursed up to INR 1,000 per month. The bill must be in the employee's name. Prepaid recharges count as mobile bills and are reimbursable only for a number registered in the employee's name.

### 6.3 Non-reimbursable items

The following are never reimbursed: alcohol and tobacco, traffic and parking fines, personal entertainment, personal grooming such as salon and spa services, personal shopping, personal mobile recharges for a number not in the employee's name, gifts to government officials, travel insurance bought separately when the Company already provides cover, and any expense the employee cannot explain. Money transferred to an individual person (a person-to-person payment) is never reimbursed. Where an individual supplier issued a proper bill, claim the bill and attach the payment as proof. Tips are reimbursable only when included on the bill and not exceeding ten percent of it.

## 7. Exceptions and audit

### 7.1 Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the department head before the expense is incurred. Retrospective exceptions are granted only in genuine emergencies and must be explained in the claim.

### 7.2 How to submit a claim

Upload each bill and, where relevant, its payment confirmation to the claims system. The system reads the bill, checks it against this policy and asks the employee only for details it could not read or that change the amount payable. Employees must review the extracted details before submitting and correct anything that is wrong; submitting a claim confirms that the information in it is accurate. A claim may contain expenses from several days of the same trip, but each bill must appear only once.

### 7.3 Audit

Finance audits a sample of approved claims every quarter. Employees must keep original bills for six months after reimbursement and produce them on request. Claims found to be false or inflated will be recovered from salary and may lead to disciplinary action under the Company's code of conduct.

### 7.4 Data and privacy

Bills and payment screenshots can contain personal information. The claims system stores them only for as long as Finance needs them for audit, restricts access to the employee, their approvers and Finance, and never uses them for any purpose other than processing and auditing expense claims. Employees should crop out unrelated personal details, such as other transactions on a bank statement, before uploading.

### 7.5 Review

Finance Controllership reviews this policy every year, and earlier if tax rules or business needs change. Questions about the policy should be sent to the Finance helpdesk.
